Progress is a horizontal bar that communicates how much of a process has completed. Pass a value between 0 and 100 to fill the bar proportionally.

## When to use

- To show determinate progress for an operation with a known duration or step count, such as an upload or import.
- To visualise completion of a quota or capacity, such as storage used.

## When not to use

- When the duration is unknown; use a spinner or skeleton instead.
- For navigation through steps; use a stepper or wizard pattern.
