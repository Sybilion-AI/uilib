Motion is quick and purposeful. Use --ease-standard for elements entering or changing state, --ease-exit for elements leaving, and pick the shortest duration that still reads: --dur-fast for hovers and toggles, --dur-base for most transitions, --dur-slow for larger surfaces.

## When to use

- Hover, focus, and state-change transitions on interactive controls.
- Entering and leaving surfaces such as popovers, sheets, and dialogs.

## When not to use

- Decorative or looping animation that does not communicate a state change.
